Privacy Policy

DD Live Tv (Android app, package com.infostream.livetv)
Effective date: 1 November 2026

In short:

  • You don't need an account. We don't ask for your name, phone number, contacts, photos or location.
  • Your favourites and settings are stored only on your device.
  • The app is free and shows ads from Google AdMob. Where the law requires it, we ask for your consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice any time in Settings › Privacy choices.
  • We use Google Firebase to understand app usage, fix crashes and send the notifications you choose.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

This Privacy Policy explains what information the DD Live Tv app ("the app", "we", "us") handles, why, and the choices you have. By using the app you agree to this policy.

DD Live Tv is an independent app. It is not run by, or affiliated with, Doordarshan, Prasar Bharati, All India Radio or any broadcaster shown in the app.

1. Information stored on your device

The following stays on your phone and is not sent to us:

  • Favourites – the channels and radio stations you save.
  • Settings – for example your notification choices and whether radio keeps playing in the background.

This data is deleted when you clear the app's data or uninstall the app.

2. Information collected by third-party services

The app uses the services below. They may collect information automatically from your device under their own privacy policies. We do not receive your name, email or phone number from them.

3. Streams, videos and news content

When you play a TV channel or radio station, your device connects directly to the broadcaster's or content provider's servers. YouTube live streams are played with YouTube's embedded player, which is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. News stories and channel lists are loaded from our content servers. These servers may receive standard connection information such as your IP address, device type and the time of the request, as is normal for any internet request.

4. Permissions

  • Internet and network state – to play streams and load channels and news.
  • Notifications – to show the alerts you choose and the radio playback controls.
  • Foreground service (media playback) – to keep radio playing when you leave the app.

The app does not request access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.

5. How we use information

  • To provide the app's features: live TV, YouTube live, radio, news, search, favourites and alerts.
  • To show ads that keep the app free.
  • To understand usage, fix crashes and improve the app.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell personal information and do not use it for purposes other than those listed here.

6. Your choices

  • Ad consent: open Settings › Privacy choices in the app to review or change your ads consent.
  • Advertising ID: on Android, go to Settings › Privacy › Ads (or Google › Ads) to reset or delete your advertising ID.
  • Notifications: choose which alerts you get in the app's Settings, or turn notifications off in Android settings.
  • Delete local data: clear the app's storage or uninstall the app.
  • Other requests: you can ask us to delete data associated with your app installation by emailing us (see Contact). Because we don't collect your name or email in the app, include any details that help us identify the request.

7. Data retention

Data on your device is kept until you remove it. Data collected by Google AdMob and Firebase is kept according to Google's retention policies; for example, Crashlytics crash reports are kept for about 90 days and Analytics data is kept for a limited period set in our Firebase project.

8. Security

Information sent between the app, our servers and Google services is transferred over encrypted connections (HTTPS) wherever the content provider supports it. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect information.

9. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, or the EU/UK GDPR), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, withdraw consent, or object to certain processing. To exercise these rights, contact us at the email below.

11. International transfers

Google's services may process information on servers outside your country. Google applies safeguards described in its privacy policy.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Please check it periodically.
